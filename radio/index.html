FIELD SPECIMEN / EMISSION ≠ RECEPTION

One sender. One receiver.

The sender constructs a short identifier with an error-detection check. It is converted into conventional dot-and-dash timing. A simulated channel may pass, damage, collide with, or entirely lose the marks. The receiving side never treats successful decoding as authenticated identity or permission.
